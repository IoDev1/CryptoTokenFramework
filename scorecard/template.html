What this is

A scoring framework for tokens that might be held through a bear market, and a planner on top of it. Each token is checked against 13 filters in three tiers: dealbreakers (1.5 points each), foundation (1.0) and context (0.5), for a total of 12. Two research passes scored the same tokens: an Opus 4.6 run and a Claude run with web search under a written rubric. Where they disagree, the table says so, and every Claude cell shows its evidence line and the sources it consulted.

How to read a score

Scores are rescaled to /12 whatever weights you set. Core hold means the token clears your threshold on your weights, Watch sits between the two thresholds, Avoid is below. Turning on hard gates drops any token that fails a dealbreaker regardless of score. Entry is a separate question from quality: the ATH distance column and the far-from-ATH filter say whether it is cheap, not whether it is good.

The data score and your judgment

The v2 score has eight quality cells. Five are computed from data every day with no model: prior-cycle survivorship, revenue level, revenue trend, supply health (with inflation measured from our own daily snapshots) and usage growth. The other three, value accrual, moat and holder concentration, plus the five gates, need judgment. Press Research prompt on any token, paste it into the model you already use, paste the JSON back with Import, and the score completes in your browser. Nothing you import leaves your device unless you share a plan link. The two reference research passes (Opus 4.6, Claude) remain as a second opinion under the 13-filter v1 scheme.

Your plan

Entry, target, stop and allocation are yours and stay in your browser. The plan panel shows what those levels imply: the market cap a target needs at today's float, the fully diluted ceiling, where that would rank today, and the portfolio multiple if every target is hit, if only Core holds hit, or if the rest go to zero. Share plan copies a link that carries your levels and weights; whoever opens it gets their own editable copy.

Not advice. Verdicts are model-generated from public sources and can be wrong. Nothing on this page recommends buying or selling anything. Check the sources, decide for yourself, and never allocate what you cannot lose.
